UN_FINDING

UN Commission finding on the Hind Rajab family and ambulance attack

The Commission investigated the killing of a family in a vehicle and two paramedics in a dispatched ambulance on 29 January 2024.

Claim recorded

The Commission concluded on reasonable grounds that the Israeli 162 Division was operating in the area and was responsible for killing the family of seven, including five children, and shelling the ambulance, killing two paramedics.

Legal characterization
UN Commission finding that the actions constituted war crimes of wilful killing and an attack against civilian objects; not a judicial judgment.
Event date
2024-01-29
Location
Gaza City, Gaza Strip
Actors

Affected population

Topics

Primary source

Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel, United Nations Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel (2024-09-11); document A/79/232, para. 95; see also para. 11.

Verification notes

Verified against paragraph 95 and the underlying factual discussion in paragraph 11 of A/79/232. Attribution and the reasonable-grounds standard are preserved; this is not presented as an individual criminal conviction.
