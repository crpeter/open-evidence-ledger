UN Commission findings on Israeli military operations in Gaza

A UN Commission of Inquiry made factual and legal findings concerning Israeli authorities' and security forces' conduct in Gaza through 31 December 2023.

Claim recorded

For the period 7 October through 31 December 2023, the Independent International Commission of Inquiry found that Israeli authorities were responsible for war crimes including starvation as a method of warfare, murder or wilful killing, intentionally directing attacks against civilians and civilian objects, forcible transfer, sexual violence, torture, and cruel or inhuman treatment; it also found crimes against humanity of extermination and gender persecution.

Legal characterization
War-crime and crime-against-humanity findings by a UN commission of inquiry; not a final judicial determination of state or individual criminal responsibility.
Event date
Not a single-date event
Location
Gaza Strip
Actors
Israeli authorities, Israeli security forces
Affected population
Palestinian civilians in the Gaza Strip
Topics
Civilian Harm, Crimes Against Humanity, Gaza, Israeli Forces, UN Commission of Inquiry, War Crimes

Primary source

Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel, United Nations Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel (2024-06-12); document A/HRC/56/26, pp. 17-18, paras. 101-105.

Short source quotation

The Commission finds that the immense numbers of civilian casualties in Gaza and widespread destruction of civilian objects and infrastructure were the inevitable result

Verification notes

Verified against the report's conclusions and its express temporal scope ending 31 December 2023 (para. 2). The Commission applied a reasonable-grounds standard described in paragraph 6. The list in this record follows paragraph 105 and is attributed only to the Commission, not characterized as a court judgment.
