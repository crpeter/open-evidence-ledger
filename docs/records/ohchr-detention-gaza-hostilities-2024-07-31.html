OHCHR report on detention in the context of Gaza hostilities

DOCUMENTED_EVIDENCE

OHCHR documented accounts and other information concerning Palestinian detention and the treatment of Israeli hostages between 7 October 2023 and 30 June 2024.

Claim recorded

OHCHR documented information that thousands of Palestinians had been taken into custody since 7 October 2023 and accounts of prolonged blindfolding, deprivation of food and sleep, beatings, electric shocks, and other ill-treatment; it also documented information concerning hostage-taking and reported ill-treatment of Israeli hostages by Palestinian armed groups.

Legal characterization
Documented evidence and accounts relevant to alleged arbitrary detention, torture or other ill-treatment, enforced disappearance, hostage-taking, and abuse; not an adjudication of individual cases.
