ICC Pre-Trial Chamber issues warrants for Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant

ICC Pre-Trial Chamber I announced arrest warrants after applying a reasonable-grounds threshold; the allegations have not been adjudicated at trial.

Claim recorded

The Chamber stated that it found reasonable grounds to believe Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant each bore criminal responsibility for the war crime of starvation as a method of warfare and crimes against humanity of murder, persecution, and other inhumane acts during the period addressed by the warrants.

Legal characterization
Arrest-warrant allegations under the Rome Statute at the reasonable-grounds-to-believe threshold; not convictions or final findings.
