ICJ advisory opinion on Israel's continued presence in the Occupied Palestinian Territory

In a final advisory opinion, the International Court of Justice concluded that Israel's continued presence in the Occupied Palestinian Territory is unlawful.

Claim recorded

The Court concluded that Israel's continued presence in the Occupied Palestinian Territory is unlawful, that Israel is obligated to bring that presence to an end as rapidly as possible, and that it must cease new settlement activity and evacuate all settlers from the territory.

Legal characterization
Final advisory opinion of the International Court of Justice; authoritative legal findings in non-binding advisory proceedings, not a contentious judgment imposing criminal liability.
