OHCHR documentation of Palestinian detainee treatment

OHCHR documented consistent accounts of treatment of Palestinian detainees between 7 October 2023 and 30 June 2024.

Claim recorded

OHCHR documented accounts from released Palestinian detainees describing prolonged blindfolding and shackling, deprivation of food and sleep, beatings, stress positions and electric shocks while in Israeli custody.

Legal characterization
Documented accounts relevant to alleged torture or other ill-treatment; not an adjudication of any individual allegation.
Event date
Not a single-date event
Location
Israel, Occupied Palestinian Territory
Actors
Israeli authorities
Affected population
Palestinian detainees
Topics
Detainees, Israeli Authorities, OHCHR, Torture

Primary source

Detention in the context of the escalation of hostilities in Gaza (7 October 2023 - 30 June 2024), Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights (2024-07-31); document OHCHR-OPT-Detention-Thematic-Report-2024-07-31, pp. 11-15, paras. 42-60.

Verification notes

Verified against the cited sections of the OHCHR thematic report, including its scope and methodology. OHCHR documents accounts and information; this record does not treat every reported incident as an adjudicated finding.
