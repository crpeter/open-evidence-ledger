ICJ interim assessment that famine was setting in

The Court assessed the deterioration in living conditions before modifying provisional measures.

Claim recorded

At the provisional-measures stage, the Court observed that Palestinians in Gaza were no longer facing only a risk of famine, but that famine was setting in.
