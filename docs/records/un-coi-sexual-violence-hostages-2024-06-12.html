UN_FINDING

UN Commission finding on sexual violence during abductions

The Commission addressed sexual and gender-based violence committed during abductions in the 7 October attacks.

Claim recorded

The Commission found reasonable grounds to conclude that members of Palestinian armed groups committed sexual and gender-based violence during the abduction of people in Israel on 7 October 2023.

Legal characterization
Factual finding by a UN commission applying a reasonable-grounds standard; it concerns violence during abduction, not treatment in captivity, and is not a judicial judgment or conviction.
Event date
2023-10-07
Location
Israel
Actors
Hamas military wing, other Palestinian armed groups
Affected population
people abducted during the 7 October attacks
Topics
7 October, Abduction, Hamas, Hostages, Sexual Violence, UN Commission of Inquiry

Primary source

Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel, United Nations Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel (2024-06-12); document A/HRC/56/26, pp. 8-9, paras. 40-44.

Verification notes

Verified against the Commission’s discussion of sexual and gender-based violence in paragraphs 40-44 of A/HRC/56/26 and its reasonable-grounds standard in paragraph 6. The claim is limited to violence during abduction on 7 October; it makes no finding about hostage treatment in captivity and does not import findings about Israeli security forces’ treatment of Palestinian detainees.
