OHCHR report on detention in the context of Gaza hostilities

UN_FINDING

OHCHR documented detention-related violations affecting Palestinians and abuses of Israeli hostages between 7 October 2023 and 30 June 2024.

Claim recorded

OHCHR reported that thousands of Palestinians had been taken into custody since 7 October 2023, many arbitrarily and in secret and in some cases subjected to treatment that may amount to torture; it also stated that Palestinian armed groups' hostage-taking and treatment of hostages entailed violations of international humanitarian and human rights law.
