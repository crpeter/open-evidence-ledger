UN Commission findings on 7 October attacks by Palestinian armed groups

UN_FINDING

A UN Commission of Inquiry found that members of Palestinian armed groups committed war crimes during their 7 October 2023 attack in Israel.

Claim recorded

The Independent International Commission of Inquiry found that members of the military wings of Hamas and other Palestinian armed groups committed war crimes on 7 October 2023, including intentionally directing attacks against civilians, murder or wilful killing, torture, inhuman or cruel treatment, and taking hostages.

Legal characterization
War-crime findings by a UN commission of inquiry; not a judicial judgment assigning individual criminal liability.
Event date
2023-10-07
Location
Israel
Actors
Hamas military wing, other Palestinian armed groups, Palestinian civilians who directly participated
Affected population
Israeli civilians, hostages, members of Israeli security forces hors de combat

Primary source

Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel, United Nations Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel (2024-06-12); document A/HRC/56/26, p. 16, paras. 96-97.

Short source quotation

The Commission concludes that members of the military wings of Hamas and of other Palestinian armed groups committed the war crimes

Verification notes

Verified against the Commission's conclusions and its methodology and evidentiary-standard sections (paras. 3-7). Paragraph 97 separately addresses Israeli civilians who directly participated. This record attributes findings to the Commission and does not present them as convictions of any named individual.
