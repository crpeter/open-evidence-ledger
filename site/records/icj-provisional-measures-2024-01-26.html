ICJ indicates provisional measures in South Africa v. Israel

COURT_INTERIM_FINDING

The International Court of Justice indicated provisional measures while expressly stating that it was not deciding the merits of South Africa's genocide claims.

Claim recorded

At the provisional-measures stage, the Court found that at least some rights South Africa claimed under the Genocide Convention were plausible and that there was a real and imminent risk of irreparable prejudice; it ordered Israel to take specified measures pending a merits decision.

Legal characterization
Provisional measures under Article 41 of the ICJ Statute in proceedings under the Genocide Convention; no final determination that genocide occurred.
