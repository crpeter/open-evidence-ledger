# Source register

This register describes the authoritative organizations represented in the pilot. Individual records—not this page—are the source of truth for document URLs, identifiers, pinpoint citations, claims, and verification notes.

## International Court of Justice

The ICJ's official case materials for *Application of the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide in the Gaza Strip (South Africa v. Israel)* are used for the Court's provisional-measures determinations. These orders must not be described as final genocide findings.

## International Criminal Court

The ICC's official situation materials are used for arrest-warrant developments in the Situation in the State of Palestine. A warrant reflects the stated statutory threshold and allegations; it is not a conviction.

## United Nations

The UN Human Rights Council's Independent International Commission of Inquiry reports are used for findings attributed to that Commission, including findings addressing Israeli authorities and Palestinian armed groups. OHCHR thematic reporting is used where its document states the basis and scope of its conclusions. UN findings are not relabeled as court findings.

## Preservation note

Official URLs can move. Contributors should retain a stable document symbol or case identifier and a page/paragraph citation, and should propose an archived official copy when one becomes available. Link status is checked independently from substantive validation.
